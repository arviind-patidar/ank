Data Sources & Intelligence Methodology

Independent & Buyer-First Position: Acre&Key does not accept developer listing fees or alter intelligence scores for commercial interest.

Primary Data Sources: Verified RERA registrations, Bengaluru Master Plan GIS overlays, Namma Metro Phase 1/2A/2B/3 alignment documentation, and live spatial coordinates.

Workplace Commute Calculation: Travel times are computed using peak-hour urban transit velocity matrix models across major tech corridors (ITPL, Outer Ring Road, Manyata, Electronic City).

Acre&Key Score Framework: Synthesizes Location (25%), Developer Track Record (20%), Product Quality (20%), Price Competitiveness vs Area Average (15%), Connectivity (10%), and Livability (10%).

Data updated: September 2026. All spatial overlays undergo monthly verification.
